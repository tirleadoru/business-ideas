LetterLyn · draft for Doru · updated 25 Nov 2026

Content & Coins

How the puzzles are organized so we can grow to thousands without redoing anything, then the rewards and prices plan (slow on purpose), the daily tasks, and how words, meanings and learning are stored. Everything here is a proposal. The open questions are at the end.

1 · Content structure

Four levels, and only the bottom one grows fast

Their 25,000 levels are 400+ chapters of about 60 puzzles, numbered in one long line. We keep the player free to choose, so we organize by subject instead, with room to grow at every level:

Shelf (specific name, exactly 15 packs)  e.g. Sea & Rivers
 └─ Pack (exactly 20 topics)              e.g. Sea Life · Coral Reef · Sharks · Whales …
     └─ Topic (3 puzzles: one per level)  e.g. "Under the sea", "Coral reef", "Sharks" …
         └─ Word list (16–18 related words)  e.g. CORAL, REEF, DIVER, SHELL, TIDE, WAVES …
  • 29 shelves, specific names decided 23 Nov: Land Animals, Birds & Bugs, Sea & Rivers, Wild Places, Plants & Gardens, Weather & Seasons, Space Adventure, Science Lab, Digital World, Everyday Food, World Kitchens, Sweets & Treats, Around the House, People & Body, Clothes & Shopping, Jobs & Trades, School Days, Sports Fan, Games & Toys, Music & Stage, Arts & Crafts, Stories & Myths, On the Move, On Vacation, City Life, Countries & Maps, Long Ago, Holidays & Festivals, Romania. A name says exactly what is inside, so no shelf ever needs a "2". The game grows by new named shelves (Birds & Bugs next to Land Animals), never by making a shelf longer. A shelf is released only when all 15 packs are done and checked.
  • Packs are narrow too. "Land Animals" is a shelf, not a pack. Narrow packs (Pets, On the Farm, Safari…) are what give variety.
  • The library is one row per shelf, swiped sideways for its packs, with a "NEW" row and a "Seasonal" row on top. A pack can sit on two shelves through tags (e.g. "Christmas food" on Food and on Holidays).
  • Pack size decided 23 Nov: exactly 20 topics (60 puzzles over the three levels), so every stamp and trophy means the same amount of play. A published pack never changes. Topics beyond 20 are kept as reserves (Extras).
  • Stamps per difficulty decided: each stamp carries a ski-style difficulty ribbon, colour plus shape so colour-blind players can read it: ● green Relaxed, ■ blue Classic, ◆ black Expert (Expert also counts for the easier ones). Stars stay for how well a puzzle was solved, metals for how many packs. Every pack stays a real challenge for heavy players, and it triples honest playable content.
  • Running out? No. The whole catalogue is written: 29 shelves, 435 packs, 8,700 topics, 26,100 pack puzzles, about 26,000 different words per language (25 Nov). At 6–8 packs a month that is 4.5–6 years of new packs, and 23 years of dailies without repeating a topic. The real limit is checking: about 1.5 h per pack per language.
  • Rewards decided: a stamp per finished pack (album) and a trophy per shelf in tiers: bronze at 5 finished packs on that shelf, silver at 10, gold at 15 = the whole shelf (changed 23 Nov; no platinum). Stamps, trophies and league medals are earned only, never sold. Each shelf has its own trophy design (a shared base with a shelf-specific top piece). The shelf's top piece looks the same at every tier; the base grows: bronze on 1 step, silver on 2 steps, gold on 3 steps plus a laurel wreath around the top piece, so colour-blind players can tell tiers apart by height. The tier name shows under it. The trophy also carries the difficulty ribbon it was earned in, so 10 packs in Expert look different from 10 in Relaxed. Gold means the whole shelf: shelves have exactly 15 packs and never grow (new subjects become new shelves), so a trophy never moves out of reach.
  • One topic serves all three difficulties. Each puzzle has 16–18 words; Relaxed uses 6–8 of them (the shortest), Classic 10–12, Expert all of them. So one written topic gives three different puzzles.
  • New languages decided: chosen by who can check them. Native-speaker "language friends" review the words, meanings and texts before a language goes live (thank-you: lifetime Plus, given from the admin site). Candidates after English and Romanian: Polish, Czech, Spanish, Italian, Hungarian.
  • Language-specific packs (Romanian cities, Romanian dishes) live next to universal ones; a universal pack is written once and translated, then checked.
  • Packs are data, not code. They already arrive through the content feed, so new packs never need an app update. After launch a published puzzle is never changed (people's progress points at it); we only add.

2 · Grid shapes

Taller grids, because phones are tall decided: we try it

You noticed their grids aren't square. Phones are about twice as tall as they are wide, so a tall grid gives bigger letters (easier to tap, easier for older eyes) than a square one with the same number of letters.

Relaxed
7 wide × 9 tall6–8 words · ~51 px letters
Classic
9 wide × 12 tall10–12 words · ~40 px letters
Expert
11 wide × 14 tall14–18 words · ~33 px letters
  • Today we use 10×10 squares (36 px letters). Classic at 9×12 has more letters and bigger ones.
  • The shape is fixed per puzzle, not per screen, so the daily and friend codes stay identical for everyone. On a computer the tall grid sits next to the word list.
  • Needs a small engine change (width and height instead of one size), and a check that the grid plus 12 words still fit on a small phone.
  • The daily decided: Classic size (9×12, all directions) every day, and on Sunday the bigger weekend special at Expert size (11×14). Same for everyone, whatever difficulty they picked for packs.
  • Game time decided: a new daily for everyone at 00:00 UTC (02:00 in Romania in winter), with a countdown on Home. Travelling never changes it. Dailies are labelled by number (Daily #326), never by weekday. The puzzle's key comes from our server at that moment, so future dailies can't be built from the code.

No patterns: how words are placed

In the other app, long words often form a lattice of parallel diagonals two rows apart. Once you see it, you follow the pattern instead of searching. Our generator today picks a random spot for each word: it never builds patterns on purpose, but nothing stops them either. The fix is to score each possible spot:

  • Mixed directions: no direction takes more than about a third of the words; Expert gets its share of backwards words.
  • No parallel neighbours: a word running the same way as another, one or two lines away, scores low.
  • Spread out: empty areas of the grid score higher, so words don't pile into one half.
  • Some crossings, not many: a few shared letters are fun; too many rebuild the lattice.
  • Then pick randomly among the best few spots, so puzzles still feel different.
  • A test generates thousands of puzzles and checks the direction mix and parallel neighbours.
  • Later, Expert only: decoy fragments in the filler letters (DREA next to DREAM).

Generator versions from launch: every phone rebuilds puzzles from a seed. If the generator improves after launch, old and new app versions would build different grids for the same daily or friend code. So each puzzle records which generator version built it, and old versions stay in the code.

3 · Rewards and prices

Slow on purpose decided 22 Nov: starting rule

Looks and collections should take some work, especially for free players. We start slow and only loosen later, never the other way round. Players notice and resent a game that gets stingier.

The pace rule

  • Nothing big in a day. No theme, accessory set or trophy can be earned in one sitting.
  • Something noticeable every 1–2 weeks for a regular free player (an accessory or frame bought with coins), a theme about every 3–4 weeks.
  • The whole launch catalogue takes a regular player ~21 months of steady income and a heavy player ~9 months, while ~6,000 coins of new items arrive every month, so the shop never runs dry (proposed 25 Nov).
  • Hints stay affordable (15 coins, ~3 puzzles). Each one delays the next look, which is the choice we want.
  • Paid content never becomes coins: Learn puzzles, Journey ticket rows and custom puzzles pay XP only, or nothing.

Rewards are coins decided 25 Nov

Playing pays coins; looks (accessories, frames, themes, room items) are bought with them, so every player picks what they want. Like Duolingo, which rewards only gems. The balance rule

  • The shop: launch catalogue ≈ 42,600 coins (6 themes ~18,000 + ~32 paid accessories ~17,600 + 10 frames ~7,000; avatars are the player's name animal, decided 25 Nov; Lyn is not dressed by players). New each month ≈ 5,900 coins (1 theme, ~4 accessories, 1 frame).
  • Nobody empties it: after the first months, a regular player earns ~2,000 a month for looks = a third of what arrives each month; a heavy player ~4,600 = 77%. Both always have something left to want, even the heavy player after buying the whole launch shop (~9 months).
  • Coins never pile up uselessly: hints, streak freezes, past dailies and name changes are used up and bought again (our version of Duolingo's energy refills, but never needed to keep playing). Later sinks: Lyn's room (many small items, cheap to make) and sending a look to a friend.
  • Slow at the start: a regular player's first theme takes ~6 weeks (was under 3), a casual player's ~6 months. A Classic puzzle pays ~5 coins; a theme is ~570 puzzles.
  • Room to loosen later: every number can only go up (rewards) or down (prices) after launch, the trust rule. Watched in the soft launch: share of players who bought nothing after 2 weeks, coins sitting unspent, hint use.
  • Model: letterlyn/tools/economy-model.py (player types, level speed: level 10 at ~1.5 weeks, 20 at ~7 weeks for a regular player).

5 · Words, meanings and the database

One concept, many languages

Today the packs live in code files and reach the apps through the content feed. To manage content from the admin site, everything moves into the database. The feed is then built from the database and the apps don't change; they still ship a built-in copy for offline first launch.

concepts     id (animal.dog, animal.bat, sport.bat), topic tags
words        concept · language · word as shown · meaning · example
             · status (draft / approved) · pronunciation (later)
shelves      id · title per language · order
packs        id · shelf · title per language · sequel of · published on
             · kind (universal / local) · learning directions
pack_words   pack · concept · which puzzle (topic)
puzzles      pack · number · difficulty · language · generator version
             · grid (frozen once live)
dailies      game day · language · puzzle · word of the day
reports      puzzle · word · reason · note · app version · status
  • Meanings are written per language, not translated, and attached to the concept, so "bat" the animal gets the right one. Our own short text (drafted offline, then checked), never copied from DEX or other dictionaries, whose definitions belong to their publishers.
  • A pack can't publish until every word has an approved meaning and example.
  • A new language = new words rows for existing concepts, plus its own local packs.
  • Learning pairs come almost free: grid from one language's words, list from the other's, same concepts. Local packs (Romanian dishes) have no pair word, so they work only in the direction whose grid is Romanian, with a description clue. Set per pack.
  • No repeats: a daily topic not again for 60 days, a word not in any daily for 90 days, the word of the day never within 365 days. Needs ≥365 approved words per language per year, which the packs cover easily.

Open questions

Decided since (22 Nov)

  • The real coin balance lives on our server. Offline earnings sync later and are re-checked against the solved puzzles; the shop needs internet.
  • Slow to earn: the pace rule, prices and examples in section 3. Paid content never turns into coins.
  • Word card and word of the day at launch; meanings are our own text, stored per concept and language.
  • Learning pair one-time ~€4.99; Learn subscription later; both stay available.
  • Earning stays capped by design (first clears pay once, Quick Play full pay 3 a day), so cheating can't farm coins. This also protects hint bundles if we ever sell them.
