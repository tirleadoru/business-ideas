Lyn character sheet and art style

Launch · first
  • Lyn = variant 11 (chosen 25 Nov by the friends' vote: 12 of 54 votes, the clear winner). The character sheet builds on that drawing.
  • Lyn from the front only: one fixed camera for every pose, with Lyn's colours and proportions (Remus, 26 Nov: in the app Lyn always faces the player, and one camera makes every prop easy to add). Side and 3/4 views are in Later, only if we ever animate Lyn or make merch.
  • Lyn has no gender (decided 22 Nov): the standard look stays neutral, and in texts Lyn is always just "Lyn". Lyn's look is ours, never dressed by players (decided 25 Nov): the standard Lyn everywhere, plus a few seasonal looks we choose.
  • A small palette and line style that everything else follows (pictures, themes, stamps).
  • Must look clear at small sizes: Lyn appears at 56–140 px on a phone.
  • Colour-blind rule for everything: never show a difference by colour alone (add a shape, label or number). Two-player colours are blue and orange, never red and green.
  • Lyn will be in the logo and app icon, so the standard look will also be registered as a figurative trademark once the logo is final. Keep that standard image stable.

Everything else depends on this. Agree on it before drawing the rest, so nothing has to be redone.

Player avatars: the name animals

32 at launchLaunch
  • Decided 25 Nov: a player's avatar is the animal in their name (CalmOtter42 → an otter), dressed with accessories. No picture list. Lyn is not dressed by players.
  • One head-and-shoulders drawing per animal in the name list: 32 today, growing to ~100 (append-only, like the list). Same size, same framing, friendly, kid-safe.
  • Each animal gets fixed anchor points: top of head, ears, eyes, nose/muzzle, neck; plus a fur layer the game can recolour and 2–3 markings. Accessories sit on these points, so one accessory drawing fits every animal (with small per-head scale/offset).
  • Must read at 32–56 px (rankings, friends).

No uploaded photos, ever. The animal + accessories is the only way players show themselves; it ties the avatar to the name.

Pack covers, shelf icons and stamps

15 per shelf + 29 + 15 per shelfLaunch
  • One cover per pack. A shelf launches complete (15 packs) once it is checked, so covers come 15 at a time. First: Sea & Rivers and Everyday Food (checked first), then Land Animals.
  • One small icon per shelf: 29 shelves (catalogue page), in the order they are checked.
  • One stamp per pack, the reward for finishing it. A simple version of the cover works.
  • Plus 3 difficulty ribbons used on every stamp and trophy, ski style, colour and shape: green circle (Relaxed), blue square (Classic), black diamond (Expert). Drawn once.

New packs arrive every month, so this is a template Remus will repeat (cover + stamp per pack).

Shelf trophies and league medals

1 base + 8 tops + 4Launch
  • One shared trophy base, plus a top piece special to each shelf (a shell and wave for Sea & Rivers, a cup for Everyday Food, a tower for City Life…). One new top per new shelf.
  • 4 states. The shelf top piece stays the same at every tier; only the metal and the base change, so colour-blind players can tell them apart by height: grey silhouette of the whole trophy (locked), bronze on a 1-step base (5 packs), silver on a 2-step base (10), gold on a 3-step base with a laurel wreath around the top piece (15 = the whole shelf). No platinum. Draw one base in 3 heights plus the laurel, shared by all shelves. Tier name shown under it.
  • A spot on the base for the difficulty ribbon.
  • League medals: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, top 10%.

Ownership

Decided 23 Nov (option B): at launch Remus transfers the copyright on Lyn and the LetterLyn art to Doru personally, in writing as Romanian law requires (rights, uses, full term, worldwide, paid or free). Remus stays credited as the author. Doru then gives SmartDevSolutions a free licence to use the name, Lyn and the art, and files the "LetterLyn" word mark and Lyn's image mark (the final variant-11 drawing) in his name.

Keep all source files in the company drive. If any stock or generated material is used, keep its licence next to the file.

The papers are made at launch and checked by a lawyer.
